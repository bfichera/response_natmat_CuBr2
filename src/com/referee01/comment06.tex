Contextualizing Magnon Excitation: On page 1, the authors state: “Conventionally, the DME is realized by coupling an external electric field to the electric dipole active pseudo-Goldstone magnon”. It should be noted that various types of magnons can be excited by oscillating electric fields. For instance, several types of electromagnons were identified in perovskite rare-earth manganese oxides over a decade ago (e.g., N. Kida et al., J. Opt. Soc. Am. B 26, A35 (2009)).
