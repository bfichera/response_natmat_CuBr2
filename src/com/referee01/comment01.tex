Correlation Between SHG Signal and Ferroelectric Polarization: I am not yet convinced that the SHG signal definitively monitors ferroelectric polarization as proposed. In the present case, where the ferroelectric polarization vector (parallel to the y-axis) lies within the incident plane, the SHG signal should theoretically appear only in the P-out channel, regardless of the incident light polarization. Consequently, the P-in/S-out and S-in/S-out channels should exhibit SHG minima at similar incident plane orientations. However, Figure 4 appears to contradict this:
• A strong SHG intensity is observed at approximately \phi = \pi/2 for the P-in/S-out channel.
• Conversely, the intensity peaks at approximately \phi = 0 for the S-in/S-out channel.
The authors should clarify this discrepancy to validate their interpretation of the SHG origin.
