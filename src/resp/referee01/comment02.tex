The rise part of the oscillation being absent is naturally accounted for by a pump-induced heating of the system.
This transient increase in temperature will quench the magnitude of ferroelectric polarization. We this expect the Higgs mode oscillation to be about this reduced mean value of polarization. 
We have updated the main text to explain this, and thank the referee for raising this important point.
