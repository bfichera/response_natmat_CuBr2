Summary of the Work
The manuscript reports on a study of time-resolved Second Harmonic Generation (SHG) performed on the layered multiferroic compound CuBr₂. The authors observe that SHG intensity oscillates following irradiation with femtosecond pulsed laser at a wavelength of 1300 nm or 1650 nm. Notably, the oscillation frequency is shown to be dependent on the polarization configuration:
• Higher-frequency oscillations are observed exclusively in the crossed polarization channels.
• Slower-frequency oscillations appear regardless of the specific polarization settings.
The authors attribute these oscillations to the spin system. Specifically, they argue that the SHG signal monitors ferroelectric polarization induced by the Cu spin cycloid. They rule out phonon-driven oscillations on the basis that known/calculated phonon frequencies are significantly higher than those observed. Ultimately, the authors ascribe the higher-frequency and lower-frequency oscillations to the anisotropy-gapped Goldstone mode and the Higgs mode of the spin system, respectively.
